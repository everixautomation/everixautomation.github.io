How it works

From inquiry to front desk in one minute.

The AI handles the first reply and the back-and-forth. Your team handles the people.

01

A lead comes in

From your website form, Facebook or Google lead ads, or any form tool you already use. Nothing changes on your site.

Any lead source
02

They get a personal reply within a minute

A short text and email in your business's voice, mentioning what they asked about and asking the best time to reach them. After hours it says "tomorrow morning", never a promise you can't keep.

Text + email · Under 60 seconds
03

The AI keeps the conversation going

It answers simple questions from your own FAQ (free consult? parking? hours?), flags hot leads who want a call or want to book this week, and passes anything personal, medical or legal straight to your team.

Only your approved answers
04

Your front desk gets a warm lead

An instant alert: name, number, what they want, and the best time to call, with tap-to-call and a "contacted" button. If nobody gets to them, the front desk gets one reminder.

🔥 Hot leads flagged first
05

Nobody slips through

Quiet leads get a check-in on day 1 and day 3, and follow-ups stop the moment they reply, book, or opt out. Every lead is logged, and you get a monthly report: response time, replies, contacted, booked.

Who it's for

Built for businesses where the first reply wins.

Each industry gets its own rules, so the AI stays in its lane.

Med Spas

Botox, filler, laser and skin inquiries answered while they're still browsing. Consults get booked before they book somewhere else.

Never discusses medical history, suitability or results. That's for the consultation.

Dental Clinics

New patients, cleanings, Invisalign and emergencies. Urgent messages are flagged hot for your team right away.

Never discusses symptoms or treatment. Urgent cases go straight to a person.

Law Firms

Real estate, family, estates, immigration and more. Every inquiry is acknowledged professionally and routed to the right person.

Never gives legal advice or predicts outcomes. It books the consult.
